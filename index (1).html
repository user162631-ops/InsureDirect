Coverage Options

Explore the coverage that fits your car

Every vehicle and coverage situation is different. Here's a quick look at common auto coverage types a licensed agent can walk you through on the phone.

Liability

Helps cover costs if you're responsible for injury or damage to others. Required in most states.

Collision

Helps pay to repair or replace your vehicle after an accident, regardless of fault.

Comprehensive

Covers non-collision events like theft, weather, fire, and vandalism.

Uninsured Motorist

Helps protect you if you're in an accident with someone who has little or no insurance.
